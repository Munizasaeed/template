Our Story

Cooked With Fire, Served With Heart

Saffron & Smoke began as a small family kitchen with one big idea: honest food made slowly. Today we still light the wood fire every morning, bake our bread in-house and build every plate around what is freshest that day.

Our chefs blend classic technique with bold spices, so each dish feels familiar and exciting at the same time. Whether it is a quiet dinner for two or a table full of family, we cook as if you were a guest in our home.
